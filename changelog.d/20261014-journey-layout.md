title: The Journey progression preset lays each expansion over its own range
---
`CoAContentScaling.Progression.Mode = Journey` compresses the old world to 1-35, Outland to 30-43 and Northrend to
40-55 by default, with the heroics and raids of Outland and Northrend at the endgame level. The ranges, the endgame
level, its boss levels, the endgame item levels and hand-set zone and instance entries are the
`CoAContentScaling.Journey.*` settings. The rules and the zone and instance tables are taken from mod-world-journey by
aspollon. The zone table now also names the era of each zone, ahead of the expansion of its map.
