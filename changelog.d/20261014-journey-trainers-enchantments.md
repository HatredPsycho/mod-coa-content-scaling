title: On the journey, riding, professions, battlegrounds, gems, enchantments and quest experience follow it too
---
Riding and profession trainers teach at the journey's levels, expert riding at the doors of Outland
(`CoAContentScaling.Journey.Trainers.Enable`). The Eye of the Storm, the Strand of the Ancients and the Isle of Conquest
open at the endgame level with their first bracket (`CoAContentScaling.Journey.Battlegrounds.Enable`). Gems and the
enchantments of Outland's and Northrend's professions keep what their game's top rewards kept of their statistics, a
socket bonus what its item kept, and the levels they ask for follow the journey
(`CoAContentScaling.Journey.Enchantments.Enable`). Quest experience takes `CoAContentScaling.Journey.Quests.XpRate` and
its per-part variants on top of the core's rate.
